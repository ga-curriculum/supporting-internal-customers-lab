<h1>
  <span class="headline">Supporting Internal Customers Lab</span>
  <span class="subhead">Instructor Guide</span>
</h1>

This lab allows students to practice supporting internal customers using a Trello board configured to simulate a ticket management system. It also emphasizes the importance of professional communication and technical writing skills.

The lab aims for students to handle internal support tickets efficiently, know when to escalate issues appropriately, and document their resolutions concisely, simulating real-world customer support workflows.

Please review the lab setup documentation to familiarize yourself with the setup process and the lab Trello resource. Consider going through the mock tickets to develop ideas for resolving them yourself. This way, you'll be better prepared for questions should students have issues getting started or working through the mock ticket resolution process.
