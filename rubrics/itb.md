## Lab requirements

The following requirements describe the content that should be present in the submitted **Supporting Internal Customers Lab** Trello board.

### Part 1 - Setup a VM - Ubuntu

| Criteria                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Points |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| Marked at least six tickets complete by moving the ticket cards from the **New Tickets** list to the **Completed**/**Requires Escalation** list.                                                                                                                              | 30     |
| Clearly describe the issue, outline assumptions, and correctly identify the potential root cause for each ticket and the steps taken to resolve the issue in the **Completed** list. Describe why escalation is required for each ticket in the **Requires Escalation** list. | 30     |
| Communication in each ticket is clear and professional.                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | 12     |
| Communication with customers is able to be understood by a non-technical audience.                                                                                                                                                                                            | 18     |
| The Trello board is publicly accessible on the first submission attempt.                                                                                                                                                                                                      | 10     |
| **Total possible points**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | 100    |
